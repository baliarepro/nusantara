Interest rates18 November 2026

Bank Indonesia raises the BI Rate by 25 bps to 6% in November 2026

Bank Indonesia's Board of Governors meeting on 17–18 November 2026 raised the BI Rate by 25 basis points to 6%. The Deposit Facility rate rose to 5.25% and the Lending Facility rate to 6.75%. According to BI Governor Destry Damayanti, the hike is meant to strengthen the stability of the rupiah, which stood at 18,245 per US dollar on 17 November 2026, 0.66% weaker than at the end of October 2026. BI said its loose macroprudential policy will continue to be strengthened to increase financing to the real sector.

Subsidised housing11 November 2026

Housing minister targets 271,000 subsidised home sales in 2026

Minister of Housing and Settlement Areas (PKP) Maruarar Sirait is targeting subsidised home sales of 271,000 units in 2026. He said sales had reached about 200,000 units as of 11 November 2026, and a mass signing of at least 71,000 homes is planned for 17 December 2026. He also said about Rp30 trillion in KUR housing credit had been disbursed as of 10 November 2026, with BRI the largest lender (Rp13 trillion) and Central Java the top province (Rp6.7 trillion). The 271,000-unit target and the mass signing are still plans.

Land15 November 2026

ATR/BPN: 35,294 land cases resolved, 100 of 120 land-mafia target operations in 2026 completed

The Ministry of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning (ATR/BPN) recorded more than 58,000 land cases over almost the past 12 years, of which 35,294 had been resolved as of 3 November 2026. The 2026 caseload consists of 21,000 cases carried over from previous years and 3,392 new cases, with 1,164 resolved. Of 120 land-mafia target operations in 2026, 100 have been completed, and assets worth about Rp5.286 trillion were saved. The figures were presented at a coordination meeting on land crime handling in Jakarta on 12 November 2026.

IKN15 November 2026

Construction of IKN's legislative and judicial areas reaches 25–32 percent

The IKN Authority recorded average physical progress of 25.40% in the legislative area and 32.83% in the judicial area. Road construction in the two areas is moving faster, at 41.49% and 46.69%. Chinese investor PT Star Bright International Investment began building at KIPP 1B in July 2026, with an investment value of Rp1.25 trillion. Construction follows Presidential Regulation No. 79 of 2025 on Phase II of IKN development for 2025–2029.

Credit11 November 2026

Government sets aside Rp30 trillion in housing credit for 2027

Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs Airlangga Hartarto said the government is preparing a housing credit allocation of Rp30 trillion for 2027, which could still be raised in the second half of 2027. The figure is part of a 2027 program credit disbursement target of Rp386.9 trillion, including KUR of Rp325 trillion, up from Rp295 trillion in 2026. House prices6 November 2026

BI survey: primary house prices up 0.58% in Q3 2026, sales down 8.43%

Bank Indonesia's Residential Property Price Survey recorded 0.58% (yoy) growth in the primary-market residential price index in Q3 2026, slowing from 0.69% in Q2. Primary home sales contracted 8.43% (yoy) and fell across all house types. Only small houses saw faster price growth, at 0.55% from 0.49%. Mortgages remained the main purchase scheme with a 70.68% share, while developers relied on internal funds for 73.94% of their financing needs.

Jakarta6 November 2026

REI: only about 116 Jakarta apartment units sold in Q3 2026

Bambang Ekajaya, Deputy Chairman of the Indonesian Real Estate Association (REI), said that according to the latest survey only about 116 apartment units in Jakarta were absorbed by the market in Q3 2026. He estimated unsold apartment stock in Jakarta at 20,000 to 25,000 units, with the upper and premium segments the weakest. REI has proposed incentives such as government-borne VAT, a BPHTB exemption and relief on service charges (IPL). These are still association proposals, not government policy.

Subsidised housing3 November 2026

FLPP subsidised mortgages reach 197,048 units in 2026, mass signings planned for December

BP Tapera recorded 194,360 FLPP mortgages worth Rp24.22 trillion as of 31 October 2026, nearly 54% of this year's 360,000-unit target. By 2 November the figure had risen to 197,048 units worth Rp24.55 trillion. Bank BTN disbursed the most (85,341 units), and West Java recorded the highest total of any province. Mass signings of 71,000 units on 17 December and 30,000 units on 21 December are being prepared.
