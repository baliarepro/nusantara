Privacy policy

What data Nusantara Property collects, how it is used, and your rights over it.

Effective date: 9 November 2026

1. Summary

Nusantara Property is a property listing platform. We only collect the data needed for agents to publish listings and for buyers to contact agents. We never sell personal data. This policy was written with reference to Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022).

2. Data we collect

  • From agents: name, office/agency, WhatsApp number, email, province and city, working area, property types, AREBI membership number (if provided), short bio, profile photo, and listing content (title, location, size, price, description, photos and Google Maps link).
  • From visitors: we do not ask for personal data. We only count, anonymously, how many times a listing is viewed and how many times its WhatsApp button is tapped, without storing who the visitor is.
  • On your device: your language choice, unsaved form drafts and a marker of listings you have viewed are kept in your own browser, not on our servers.

3. How we use data

  • To show listings and agent profiles to buyers on the website.
  • To connect buyers with agents on WhatsApp.
  • To promote listings on Nusantara Property's official social media accounts (such as Instagram), including the agent's name and WhatsApp link.
  • To send service emails to agents, such as reminders before a listing expires and account confirmations.
  • To review agent registrations and keep listing quality high.

4. What is public

Agent names, offices, working areas, bios, photos and all published listing content are public. Agents' WhatsApp numbers power the contact buttons. Agents' email addresses are never shown publicly.

5. Storage and third parties

Data is stored with the Supabase database service. Service emails are sent through Resend. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Promoted listings are uploaded to Instagram, owned by Meta. Each service has its own privacy policy. We do not share personal data with anyone else, except where required by law.

6. How long we keep data

  • Listings not updated for 60 days are removed from public view.
  • Listings marked sold or rented are permanently deleted 7 days later, with their photos.
  • Agent account data is kept while the account is active and deleted when the agent asks for the account to be removed.

7. Your rights

As the data owner you may request a copy of your data, correct inaccurate data, withdraw consent, and ask for your data and account to be deleted. Most data can be changed yourself in the agent dashboard. For anything else, contact the admin on WhatsApp.

8. Security

Access is restricted: each agent can only change their own listings, and only the admin can manage all data. No system is completely immune, however. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you as required by law.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest effective date is always shown on this page.
