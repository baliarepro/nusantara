Welcome

Nusantara Property is a property listing platform for agents across Indonesia. Buyers see your listings and contact you directly on WhatsApp, with no extra middleman.

  • Your own listing pages and agent profile, with a WhatsApp button to your number.
  • Found on Google: every listing has its own page, in Indonesian and English.
  • Promoted on Instagram @nusantara.properties, with your name and WhatsApp link in the caption.
  • Statistics: how many times a listing is viewed and how many times its WhatsApp button is tapped.
  • Automatic email reminders before a listing expires.
  • Automatic watermark on every photo, so it is harder to copy.

How to register

Registration takes about 5 minutes; the admin then reviews your account before you can post listings.

  1. Open the ☰ menu and choose Join as an agent.
  2. Enter your email, a password (at least 8 characters), full name, office or agency (leave blank if independent), active WhatsApp number, province, city and specialist areas.
  3. Tick the property types you market, write a short bio, and accept the Terms of use.
  4. Tap Create account, then click the confirmation link in your email. Check Spam if it isn't in your inbox.
  5. Wait for admin approval. You'll get an email when your account is approved.

Tip: install the app on your phone. In Chrome tap ⋮ then Install app. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Listing rule: Direct Owner

Every listing must be one we can stand behind for buyers. So:

  • Only list properties you received directly from the owner (Direct Owner), with the owner's permission to market them.
  • Don't list properties passed on by other brokers, including DKO, and don't copy ads, photos or descriptions from other sites.
  • Make sure price, size, land title, zoning and location are accurate.
  • Update or mark as sold promptly when price or availability changes.

When saving a listing you must tick the Direct Owner statement. Visitors can report incorrect listings, and listings that don't comply are taken down by the admin.

How to post a listing

  1. Open the Agent dashboard and tap Add listing.
  2. Fill in the title, type and status (for sale or for rent).
  3. Fill in the location: province, city or regency, and district or estate name.
  4. Enter the land size, plus building size, bedrooms and bathrooms for buildings.
  5. Choose the land title and zoning. Write the facing freely, e.g. "East" or "Corner East & South".
  6. Enter the price (thousands separators are added automatically) and its unit: Total, Per are, Per m², Per year (rent) or Per are per year (land lease).
  7. For land, paste the Google Maps link: open the location in Google Maps, tap Share and copy the link.
  8. Write the description: road access and width, view, nearby facilities and distances to key places.
  9. Upload up to 5 photos via Phone gallery / camera or Google Drive / files. The first photo is the cover.
  10. Tick the Direct Owner statement, then tap Save listing.

Unsaved entries are restored automatically if the app closes midway.

Tips for listings that get noticed

  • Cover photo: your most attractive, bright, landscape shot. Shoot in the morning or late afternoon.
  • Photo order: cover, road access, the whole plot or building, then the view or details.
  • Title: type, size, location and one selling point, e.g. "4.3 are land in Ungasan near Melasti Beach".
  • Price: show a clear price. Listings without a price get fewer enquiries.
  • Description: the facts buyers ask on site, such as road width, land title, zoning and distance to the beach or main road.

Managing listings

Listings not updated for 60 days are removed from the website automatically.

  • 7 days and 1 day before, you get a reminder email. Tap Republish if the property is still available.
  • Sold or rented? Tap Mark as sold or Mark as rented. The listing leaves search and is permanently deleted 7 days later; within those 7 days you can still tap Undo sold.
  • Statistics: the dashboard shows views and WhatsApp taps per listing. Many views but few enquiries usually means the price, photos or title need work.

Promotion and sharing

  • Instagram @nusantara.properties: live listings are posted as a carousel, with your name and WhatsApp link in the caption.
  • Share and Copy link on the listing page. The link shows the photo, title and price when pasted into WhatsApp.
  • Download PDF brochure to send to prospective buyers.

Common questions

Can other agents change my listings? No. Only you and the admin can edit or delete them.

Is my email public? No. Buyers only see your name, office, bio and WhatsApp button.

Forgot your password? On the Agent sign-in page, tap Forgot password.

Delete your account? Contact the admin on WhatsApp.
