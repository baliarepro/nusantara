Problems in the field, and our answers

Listings scattered across groups

What often happens

One property circulates in dozens of WhatsApp groups with different prices and details, until buyers can't tell which is right.

On Nusantara Property

Every listing has one page and one responsible agent. Buyers know exactly who to contact.

Old ads never come down

What often happens

The property sold long ago, but the ad keeps circulating for months, wasting buyers' and agents' time.

On Nusantara Property

Listings not updated for 60 days are taken down automatically. Agents are reminded a week before to republish if the property is still available.

Key details are often missing

What often happens

A buyer's first questions on site are always the same: what title, what zoning, which way it faces, where exactly it is.

On Nusantara Property

The listing form asks for what buyers actually ask: title, zoning, size in m² and are, facing, building size, rooms, and a Google Maps pin for land.

Hard to judge the agent

What often happens

Buyers can't tell a serious agent from an unclear middleman.

On Nusantara Property

Every agent is reviewed by an admin before their profile and listings go live, complete with office, working area and experience.

Started in Bali, for all of Indonesia

Nusantara Property was founded by a property practitioner active in Bali since 2009, focused on land and property in South and North Kuta. More than 15 years in the field, from land plots on the Bukit to villas in Canggu, shaped how this platform works: information must be honest, complete and current.

That experience is now open to agents in other regions. Any property agent who works the same way can join, manage their own listings, and connect directly with buyers across Indonesia.
