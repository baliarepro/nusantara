FAQ

Frequently asked questions

For buyers

Does Nusantara Property sell property itself?

No. Every listing is posted by a registered property agent. You deal and transact directly with that agent.

How do I ask about a listing?

Open the listing page and tap Ask on WhatsApp. A message with the listing title and location is filled in automatically to the agent's WhatsApp.

Why do some listings say "Price on request"?

The agent chose not to show the price on the website. Ask the agent for the latest price.

What do the zone colours on land listings mean?

The colours follow spatial planning maps: pink for tourism, yellow for residential, green for agricultural and red for trade and services. Always confirm the official zoning with the local planning office before buying.

Are the listings here guaranteed to be legally sound?

Agents are verified by an admin before they can post, but checking a property's legal status remains the buyer's responsibility. Follow the steps in our Guide and use a PPAT/notary.

For agents

How do I sign up as an agent?

Tap Agent sign-up, fill in your details and create a password, then confirm your email. Once an admin approves your account, your profile goes live and you can post listings from the Dashboard.

I didn't receive the confirmation email.

Check your Spam or Promotions folder. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, contact the admin on WhatsApp.

I forgot my password.

Open the Sign in page, enter your email and tap Forgot password. A link to set a new password will be sent to your email.

Can other agents change my listings?

No. Each listing can only be edited or deleted by its own agent and the admin.

Why is my listing no longer showing?

Listings are taken down automatically if not updated for 60 days, so buyers only see properties that are still available. A warning appears on your Dashboard a week before. Tap Republish on the Dashboard and the listing shows again for the next 60 days.

How do I hide a listing without deleting it?

Edit the listing, untick Publish this listing on the website, and save. It becomes a draft you can publish again at any time.

How many photos can I upload?

Up to 5 photos per listing. The first photo is the cover, and photos are resized automatically to keep the website fast.
